LVM3 (formerly GSLV Mk III) is short and wide where PSLV is tall and slender. ISRO
lists height 43.5 m, liftoff mass 640 t, about 4,000 kg to GTO and 8,000 kg to LEO.
First flight was the suborbital CARE mission on 18 December 2014; the first orbital
flight followed on 5 June 2017. It has launched Chandrayaan-2/3 and commercial
constellation missions, and is the basis for HLVM3 — the human-rated variant planned
to carry the Gaganyaan crew module.
