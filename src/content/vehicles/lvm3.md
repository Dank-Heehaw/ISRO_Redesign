LVM3 is short and wide where PSLV is tall and slender, because its mass is in two
S200 solid boosters flanking a liquid core. It launched Chandrayaan-3, flies
commercial constellation missions, and is the basis for HLVM3 — the human-rated
variant that will carry the Gaganyaan crew module.
