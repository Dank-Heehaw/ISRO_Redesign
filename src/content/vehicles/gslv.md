GSLV Mk II is defined by its third stage. When access to Russian cryogenic engines
was blocked, the alternative was to develop the CE-7.5 domestically — a decade of
work on a propulsion technology that only a handful of countries possessed. The
vehicle now flies operationally, and lofted NISAR in 2025.
