---
title: First national workshop on microgravity research convenes
date: 2026-05-30
type: science
summary: >-
  Researchers gathered to shape India's microgravity experiment portfolio,
  building on results returned from the Axiom-4 mission.
---

The workshop set priorities for the Indian Microgravity Experiments programme,
drawing on the experiment set flown by Gaganyatri Shubhanshu Shukla aboard the
International Space Station.
