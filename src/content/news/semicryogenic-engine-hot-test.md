date: 2026-05-08

summary: >-
  The Power Head Test Article of the SCE-200 semicryogenic engine has been hot
  tested at an elevated thrust level, advancing next-generation propulsion.
---

Semicryogenic propulsion using refined kerosene and liquid oxygen offers higher
thrust and lower cost than the storable propellants it would replace on future
heavy launch vehicles.
