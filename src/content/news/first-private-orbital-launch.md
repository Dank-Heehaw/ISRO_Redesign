---
title: First private orbital launch lifts off from Sriharikota
date: 2026-06-11
type: launch
summary: >-
  An Indian private operator has conducted an orbital launch from the Satish
  Dhawan Space Centre, a first for the country's commercial sector.
---

The launch marks the point at which India's space sector reform moves from policy
to flight hardware, with a private operator using national range infrastructure
for an orbital mission.
