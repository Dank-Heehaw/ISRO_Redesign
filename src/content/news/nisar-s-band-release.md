title: NISAR S-Band SAR data products released to the public
date: 2026-07-20
type: science
summary: >-
  Calibrated S-band products from the NISAR mission are now available through
  Bhoonidhi, including soil moisture at 100 m resolution.
featured: true
---

The S-band synthetic aperture radar contributed by ISRO to the joint NISAR
mission has completed calibration and validation, and its data products are now
open to researchers and operational users.
