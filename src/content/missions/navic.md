NavIC — Navigation with Indian Constellation — exists because relying on a
navigation system someone else can switch off is a strategic liability. Seven
satellites in geosynchronous and geostationary orbits provide continuous regional
coverage without needing a global constellation's thirty-plus spacecraft.

The system broadcasts a standard positioning service open to civilians and a
restricted service for authorised users. Newer satellites in the series add an
L1 signal, which matters commercially: L1 is the band consumer chipsets already
receive, so NavIC support stops requiring specialised hardware.

IRNSS-1F completed a ten-year mission life in 2025, and second-generation NVS
satellites are progressively replacing the original constellation.
