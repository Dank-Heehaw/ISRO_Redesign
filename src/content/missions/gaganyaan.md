Gaganyaan is the programme that changes what ISRO is. Every prior mission could
accept the loss of a vehicle as a bad day; this one cannot. Human rating the LVM3
means re-qualifying propulsion, structures, avionics and abort systems against a
standard the organisation has never had to meet.

Qualification has advanced on several fronts at once. The Crew Escape System has
been demonstrated in flight, the Service Module Propulsion System has completed
hot tests, and the Integrated Main Parachute Airdrop Test series has proven the
deceleration sequence that brings the crew module to a survivable splashdown.

The uncrewed G1 flight is the gate. Human-rated LVM3 assembly for it has begun at
Sriharikota, with the robotic Vyommitra aboard in place of a crew.
